    INSURANCE & RENTAL INFORMATION

    Insurance

    Comprehensive · excess up to €300

    • Comprehensive insurance
    • Excess up to €300
    • No security deposit or guarantee is required for the insurance excess.
    • For a single-vehicle accident caused by the driver, with no other vehicle involved, liability may be up to €300 depending on the damage
    • Coverage applies when traffic rules are followed

    What Is Not Covered

    When cover does not apply

    • Alcohol or drugs
    • Racing
    • Off-road / unpaved roads
    • Illegal use
    • Serious traffic violations
    • Unauthorized driver
    • Ferry transport without permission
    • Interior damage not caused by collision
    • Lost/damaged key
    • Vehicle kept beyond the agreed rental period

    Driver Requirements

    Age 23 · licence 1 year+

    • Minimum age: 23
    • Convertibles & larger vehicles: 25+
    • Driving licence held for at least 1 year

    Rental & Payment

    08:30–21:00 · cash or card

    • Rental day: 08:30–21:00
    • Payment by cash or card
    • No security deposit or guarantee is required for the insurance excess.

    Additional Driver

    Allowed · no extra charge

    • Additional driver allowed
    • No additional charge
    • Driver must meet the applicable driving requirements

    Vehicle Use

    Roads, smoking and return

    • No smoking
    • No dirt or unpaved roads
    • No ferry transport to another island without permission
    • Follow all traffic rules and posted signs
    • Traffic fines are the renter’s responsibility
    • Return the vehicle with the interior clean

    Cancellation

    Free until 1 day before

    • Free cancellation up to one day before rental start
    • Later cancellations are subject to the applicable rental charge
